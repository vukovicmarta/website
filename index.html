Marta Vukovic

How much democracy are citizens willing to give up, and what makes them fight to keep it? I am a Senior Scientist at the Department of Government, University of Vienna, and my research examines the strength, fragility and limits of citizens' commitment to liberal democracy.

I explore which liberal democratic principles citizens hold most dear, which they are willing to compromise, and where they draw the line, with a particular focus on how elite framing and political cues shape their democratic commitments and choices.
